## 📝 Project Overview

The Delivery Boy Application for CS-Cart helps store owners manage and track last-mile deliveries through a dedicated mobile application and admin panel. Delivery staff can accept active orders, manage deliveries, track order history, use navigation, and complete deliveries with OTP verification.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* Delivery boy profile and account management
* Active order management
* Order acceptance and delivery tracking
* Google Maps navigation
* OTP-based delivery verification
* Cash-on-delivery collection
* Earnings and withdrawal tracking
* Delivery boy ratings and reviews
* Order history and canceled order management
* Admin dashboard for delivery activity and reporting
